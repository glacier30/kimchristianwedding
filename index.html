Our Story

How it began

A few chapters from the years that brought us here — from a chance meeting to the promise we're about to make.

2017

Where It All Began

Christian and Kim met in college, and from the beginning, they were complete opposites.

Christian was quiet, calm, and reserved. He preferred to listen and observe, but behind his nonchalant personality was someone thoughtful, respectful, and dependable.

Kim, on the other hand, was outgoing, naturally funny, and could talk to just about anyone. She was confident, warm, and full of life, an institute scholar and Cum Laude graduate.

Despite their differences, they found themselves drawn to each other, and after graduation, they decided to take a chance on their growing feelings.

2018 Onwards

Growing Together

As they spent more time together, they discovered more about each other.

Christian admired Kim’s kindness and compassion, especially the way she cared for her family and grandparents. Kim, in turn, came to appreciate Christian’s patience, sincerity, and steady presence.

Their differences became part of what made them work. Kim brought laughter and energy into Christian’s life, while Christian brought calm and stability into hers.

Through the years, they supported each other through life’s changes and continued to grow together.

2025

A Promise for Forever

After years together, with their families settled, Christian knew it was time to take the next step.

At the beautiful Crosswinds in Tagaytay City, he asked Kim the most important question of his life.

And, of course, she said YES!

For Kim, Christian was the man she had prayed for, someone who loved her genuinely, respected her, supported her craziness, and remained by her side through the years.

For Christian, Kim had become more than the funny girl he met in college. She was the woman he deeply loved and wanted to spend the rest of his life with.

2026

Forever Begins

After more than a year of planning and preparing, Christian and Kim are finally ready to make their greatest promise to each other.

On December 4, 2026, they will officially begin their forever as husband and wife.

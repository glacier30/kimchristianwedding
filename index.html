Our Story

Right in front of us the whole time

"Our story is proof that sometimes the person you're meant to be with has been right in front of you the whole time."

They say the best love stories sneak up on you — and ours definitely took its time. Kim and Christian first crossed paths at St. Dominic Institute, back when neither of us had a clue we were looking at our future spouse. We were just two students surviving exams, group projects, and questionable cafeteria food. Somewhere between all that chaos, a friendship formed — the kind that didn't arrive with fireworks, just easy conversations, inside jokes, and the quiet sense that this person simply got it.

Fast forward past graduation, growing up, and life doing its thing — and somehow, we found our way back to each other. Turns out, the person we were meant to be with had been standing right there the whole time. We just needed a few years (and a little bit of luck) to catch up.

These days, our favorite thing to do together is explore — new cities, new restaurants, and road trips with questionable GPS directions. If there's a place we haven't been, consider it added to the list. That same spirit of adventure is exactly what made Tagaytay the perfect backdrop for our engagement: cool breeze, incredible view, and Christian down on one knee. (Kim said yes, in case that wasn't already obvious.)

We're just two best friends who love wandering the world together — and now we finally get to do it as husband and wife. Thank you for being part of our story so far. We can't wait to celebrate the next chapter with you.
